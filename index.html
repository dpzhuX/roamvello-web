Everything about your trip, in one place

From the first plan to the last photo, Roamvello helps you remember not just where you went, but everything that made the trip special.

Plan your journey

  • Plan stops, expected costs and tickets day by day, with a calendar and map.
  • Get ready with reusable checklists for packing, documents and more, with progress and deadlines.

Capture every moment

  • Drop a marker to save the time and add nearby place details.
  • Record hikes, rides, runs and drives with a live map, distance, speed and elevation.
  • Save voice memos, expenses in any currency, and ticket-style records of flights, trains, museums and parks.

Explore and remember

  • Follow your trip on a map with numbered stops, routes and recorded trails.
  • Collect countries and regions, cities, and U.S. and Canadian national parks.
  • Export your map, tickets, timeline and expenses together as one picture.

Your trips stay yours

Roamvello is built so that your travel history lives on your iPhone, not on someone else's server.

No account

No sign-up, no login, no email address. Open the app and start planning.

Stored on your device

Trips, trails, voice memos, expenses and tickets are saved locally on your iPhone.

No tracking or ads

No analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs watching how you use the app.

You hold the backup

Export a backup file and keep it wherever you choose. Import it to restore your trips.
